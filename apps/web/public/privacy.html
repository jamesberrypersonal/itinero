Privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026

Itinero is a trip planner: you build a trip's itinerary day by day, keep its bookings, and share it with the people you travel with. Itinero is run by James Berry. This policy says what Itinero holds about you, where it is held, who can see it, and how long it is kept.

In short: Itinero holds only what it needs to sign you in and to keep your trips. It shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking, and never sells or shares your information.

What Itinero holds

  • Your Google account's identity. When you sign in with Google, Itinero receives your account's identifier, your email address, whether Google has verified it, and your name. It asks for nothing else, and has no access to your Gmail, contacts, files or calendar.
  • The name you choose. Other travellers on your trips see you by a name you choose, starting from your Google account's name. You can change it at any time.
  • Your trips. Everything you and the other travellers enter on a trip: its itinerary, pages, bookings and calendar, and who is on it.
  • Invitations. When an admin invites someone to a trip, Itinero keeps the email address they entered, the access they offered, and whether the invitation was accepted.
  • Your session. A cookie keeps you signed in on each device. Itinero stores only a digest of it, with the time it was last used.

What stays on your device

  • Where you are. If you ask Itinero to find the country you are in, your device works that out itself. Your location never leaves your device.
  • A copy of your trips, so you can read them with no connection, and the lists of places Itinero uses to fill in time zones. Signing out deletes the copy.

Who can see it

  • The travellers on a trip see your chosen name, and what the trip's admins let them see of it. An admin also sees the email address of each traveller on the trip.
  • Itinero's service providers, who handle it only to run Itinero: Cloudflare, which serves Itinero and stores your account and trips; Google, which signs you in; and Resend, which sends invitation emails.

No one else. Itinero does not sell, rent or share your information for advertising.

Information from Google

Itinero uses the information it receives from Google only to sign you in, to show your name to the travellers on your trips, and to match invitations to your email address. Itinero's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long it is kept

  • A session is deleted when you sign out, or after 180 days unused.
  • Your account, and what you have entered on trips, are kept until you ask for them to be deleted.
  • If you are removed from a trip, you lose access to it on every device, and your name stays on it only marked as removed.

Your choices

  • You can change the name other travellers see at any time.
  • You can ask for your account and your information to be deleted, or for a copy of it, by emailing the address below.
  • You can remove Itinero's access to your Google account at any time, from your Google account's connections.

Children

Itinero is not meant for children under 13, and does not knowingly hold their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with its date.

Contact

For any question about your information, or to ask for it to be deleted, email privacy@useitinero.ca.
