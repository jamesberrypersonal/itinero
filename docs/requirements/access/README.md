| [membership.md](membership.md) | Who is on each trip: its admins, the invitations that bring travellers in, and removing them | Against the component alone, with an email stand-in that keeps each message |
| [permissions.md](permissions.md) | What each traveller may see and change of a trip's pages, calendar and bookings | Against the package alone, with a trip through Chile and trees of up to a thousand pages |
