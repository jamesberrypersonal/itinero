# Bookings requirements

What the bookings system and its components promise.

| Document | Promises about | Verified |
|---|---|---|
| [bookings.md](bookings.md) | A trip's bookings, such as flights, stays and tours, each for the travellers it names, and the page that lists them | End to end, on a running system, with a trip through Chile and a phone taken offline |
| [activity.md](activity.md) | A tour, a ticket or a table, at one place, on a date and perhaps at a time | Against the package alone, with tours, tickets and tables in several time zones |
| [flight.md](flight.md) | A flight, in one or more legs, each leaving and arriving in its own airport's local time | Against the package alone, with flights of one and several legs across time zones |
| [journey.md](journey.md) | A train, a bus, a ferry or a hired car, from one place to another, with a time at each end | Against the package alone, with trains, buses, ferries and hired cars across time zones |
| [list.md](list.md) | A trip's bookings: adding, changing and removing each one, the travellers it names, and the order they happen in | Against the package alone, with bookings of every kind, under two device time zones |
| [stay.md](stay.md) | One or more nights' stay, such as a hotel or a flat, from check-in to check-out | Against the package alone, with stays of every precision of check-in and check-out |
